About Us

A Trusted Bridge Between Philippine Healthcare Talent and American Care Teams

Fil-Am Healthcare Solutions is a healthcare recruitment agency dedicated to connecting highly trained Filipino nurses, caregivers, CNAs, and allied health professionals with hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, and home health agencies across the United States.

We manage every stage of the journey — credentialing, licensure, visa sponsorship, and relocation — so healthcare facilities gain dependable, work-ready talent, and professionals gain the career they have trained years to earn.
