About Fil-Am Healthcare Solutions

Fil-Am Healthcare Solutions (FAHS) is a healthcare recruitment agency built around a simple idea: talented Filipino healthcare professionals deserve a real path to meaningful careers in the United States, and U.S. care teams deserve dependable, compassionate staff they can count on.

We handle the recruiting, credentialing, visa, and relocation work behind every placement so both sides of that partnership can stay focused on what matters most — patient care.

Basic Info

What Fil-Am Healthcare Solutions Does

A quick summary of who we are, who we serve, and where we operate.

What We Do

FAHS is a healthcare recruitment agency that sources, credentials, and places skilled professionals into full-time roles — managing every step from first application to first day on the floor.

Who We Serve

Filipino nurses, caregivers, CNAs, and allied health professionals, placed with U.S. hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, and home health agencies.

U.S. Headquarters

Founded and headquartered in the United States, FAHS was built from day one to serve as a direct link between American employers and Filipino healthcare talent.

Philippine Office

Based in Iloilo, Philippines, our on-the-ground team supports candidate sourcing, screening, and onboarding close to home.

What Drives Us

Vision, Mission & Our Goal

Three ideas guide every placement we make — where we're headed, why we do this work, and what we're building toward next.

Vision

We envision a future where distance is no longer a barrier between talent and opportunity — where FAHS is the trusted bridge every Filipino healthcare professional turns to for a U.S. career, and every American care team turns to for dependable, compassionate staff.

Mission

Our mission is to connect skilled, compassionate Filipino nurses, caregivers, CNAs, and allied health professionals with U.S. hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, and home health agencies — and to support every step of that journey with hands-on credentialing guidance, visa sponsorship, and relocation assistance.

Our Goal

Our near-term goal is measurable growth: increasing the number of healthcare professionals we place each year, expanding our employer network into new states and care settings, and deepening long-term staffing partnerships with the facilities that rely on us most.
