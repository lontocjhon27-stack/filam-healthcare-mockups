Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Values

The principles that guide every placement, partnership, and conversation at FAHS.

Our Mission

To connect skilled, compassionate Filipino healthcare professionals with U.S. facilities that need trusted talent, guiding every step from credentialing to their first day on the floor.

Our Vision

A future where every qualified Filipino healthcare professional has a clear, supported path to a rewarding career in the United States.

Our Values

  • Integrity — Honest guidance at every step of the journey.
  • Compassion — Treating every candidate and client like family.
  • Excellence — Rigorous standards behind every placement we make.
  • Partnership — Long-term relationships built on trust and results.
