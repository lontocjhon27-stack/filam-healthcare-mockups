What Our Legal Partner Handles

From the first petition filing to ongoing status monitoring, our legal partner manages the compliance details so candidates and facilities do not have to.

EB-3 & H-1B Visa Petition Filing

Preparing and filing employment-based visa petitions so every credentialed candidate has a clear, compliant path to work in the United States.

Case Documentation & Filing Management
