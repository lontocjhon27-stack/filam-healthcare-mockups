Support FAQ

Common Support Questions

Reach out to your assigned case manager by phone or email with your full name and application date, and they will confirm exactly where things stand. If you haven’t been assigned a case manager yet, contact our general support line and we’ll look up your record.

Visa and immigration questions are handled by our in-house immigration support team. Email or call our support line and mention that your question is visa-related so it can be routed directly to that team.

Contact your case manager or account manager as soon as possible and describe what happened. Placement issues are treated as a priority, and support will follow up with next steps and, if needed, connect you with the right specialist team.

Most support requests receive a response within one to two business days. Placement issues and urgent visa matters are prioritized and typically receive a faster response.

Email your case manager with your updated phone number, address, or documents attached. They will confirm your file has been updated and let you know if anything else is needed.

Your dedicated account manager handles billing and contract questions directly. If you’re not sure who your account manager is, reach out through our employer inquiry page and support will connect you.
