What To Expect

What Happens After You Apply

A clear, guided sequence so you always know what comes next — from the moment your application lands in our system to your first day on a U.S. care team.

1

Application

You submit your profile, background, and required documents.

2

Initial Screening

A case manager reviews your application for completeness and fit within 2 business days.

3

Interview

We talk through your experience, goals, and availability to understand the right fit.

4

Credential Verification

Your education, license, and work history are verified against source documents.

5

Licensing / NCLEX

We confirm your exam status and guide any remaining licensure steps.

6

Employer Matching

You're introduced to partner facilities that fit your profession, experience, and location preferences.

7

Job Offer

The employer extends a formal offer once both sides agree on terms.

8

Visa / Immigration Processing

Our team files your visa petition and manages the required paperwork.

9

Relocation

We help coordinate housing, travel, and arrival logistics ahead of your start date.

10

Placement

You start your role, with a dedicated FAHS contact staying engaged through your first year.
